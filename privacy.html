Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Introduction

FlipVPN ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our VPN application.

No-Log Policy

We operate under a strict no-log policy. We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Your browsing history or traffic data
  • DNS queries
  • IP addresses connected to our servers
  • Connection timestamps or session duration
  • Bandwidth usage per user

Information We Collect

We collect minimal, non-personal information necessary to operate the service:

  • Crash reports: Anonymous crash data to improve app stability
  • App performance: Aggregated, anonymized analytics to optimize our servers

Advertising

Our free version is supported by ads provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may collect anonymized device identifiers for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

Data Security

All VPN connections are encrypted using WireGuard protocol with state-of-the-art cryptographic standards. Your traffic is fully encrypted end-to-end.

Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services
  • CAS (CleverAdsSolutions) for ad mediation
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@flipvpn.app.
